## 👤 About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineer from Kerala who ended up loving the unglamorous middle of data science: cleaning messy inputs, wiring together scrapers and models, and shipping something a non-technical person can actually click through. My final-year project on sign language recognition got published at an **IEEE international conference** and won **Best Paper**, and my first job out of college wasn't a typical internship — I was the **System Administrator for a state government's AI camera surveillance project**, keeping 100+ live cameras healthy in production. Since then I've been building end-to-end ML products on my own: sentiment engines, document-AI pipelines, forecasting dashboards, and a few things I just wanted to see if I could build.

I like problems where the data is ugly, the deadline is real, and the output has to make sense to someone who isn't a data scientist.

- 🔭 Building out a suite of NLP/sentiment tools across different platforms (YouTube, X, Google Reviews, raw text)
- 🌱 Deepening my production ML/MLOps skills — Docker, proper deployment pipelines, model monitoring
- 🗣️ Also certified in French (B1/B2) — because apparently I like parsing languages, human or otherwise
- 💬 Ask me about anomaly detection, RoBERTa-based sentiment pipelines, or keeping 100 cameras alive at 2 a.m.
